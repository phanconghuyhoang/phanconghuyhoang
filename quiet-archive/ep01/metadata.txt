TITLE:
Three Lighthouse Keepers Vanished in 1900. Here's What the Records Actually Say

DESCRIPTION:
In December 1900 the light on Eilean Mòr in the Flannan Isles went dark, and when the relief ship finally arrived eleven days later, all three keepers were gone. This episode tells the story using only what the people who were there wrote down, then separates the documented facts from the legend that grew around them: the fake logbook, the uneaten meal, the ghost ships.

DOCUMENTED vs LEGEND
Documented: the light went out on the night of 15 December 1900; the station work was done that day; two sets of oilskins were missing and one was left behind; the west landing showed severe storm damage about 33 m (110 ft) above the sea; no bodies were recovered.
Legend: the dramatic logbook entries, the uneaten meal and overturned chair, sea serpents, spies and ghost boats.
