DOCUMENTED vs LEGEND
Documented: the light went out on the night of 15 December 1900; the station work was done that day; two sets of oilskins were missing and one was left behind; the west landing showed severe storm damage about 33 m (110 ft) above the sea; no bodies were recovered.
Legend: the dramatic logbook entries, the uneaten meal and overturned chair, sea serpents, spies and ghost boats.
