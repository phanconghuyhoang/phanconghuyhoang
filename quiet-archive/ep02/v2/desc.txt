In January 1921 the five-masted schooner Carroll A. Deering ran aground on Diamond Shoals off Cape Hatteras with every sail set and not a single person aboard. Her first mate had been heard threatening the captain. A red-haired stranger spoke for the ship at the last lightship she passed. Five federal departments investigated, and nobody ever found the eleven men. This video follows the record, then separates what is documented from what was added later.

DOCUMENTED vs LEGEND
Documented: the mate's threat in Barbados; the red-haired man at the Cape Lookout lightship; the grounding on Diamond Shoals with sails set; wheel and compass stand wrecked; log, instruments, belongings and both lifeboats gone; food apparently prepared in the galley; the bottle note was a confessed hoax; the inquiry closed in 1922 with no finding.
Legend: the exact meal on the stove, the cat that supposedly survived, anything supernatural.
