In 1962 a fire got into the abandoned coal mines under Centralia, Pennsylvania. Nobody could put it out. Two decades later the government paid to move almost the entire town, the state took the land, and the Postal Service even revoked its ZIP code. The fire is still burning. Archie follows the records from the landfill fire to today, and explains the town's real connection to Silent Hill.

DOCUMENTED vs LEGEND
Documented: a fire in the coal mines under Centralia from about 1962; failed attempts to extinguish it; Todd Domboski's 1981 fall into a sinkhole with lethal carbon monoxide; more than $42 million allocated by Congress in 1983 for buyouts; eminent domain in 1992; ZIP code 17927 discontinued in 2002; 2013 settlement with the last residents; 5 residents in the 2020 census; the fire still burns.
Disputed: exactly how it started (landfill burn, hot ash, or an older mine fire).
Legend: that Centralia is cursed, or that the Silent Hill games were based on it (the 2006 film's screenwriter researched it).
