{a:point_l/neutral}{t:A FIRE UNDERGROUND} Now, here's why a coal mine fire is a nightmare. It is not like a house fire.
{a:talk/neutral} Underground, the fire can creep slowly along the coal seams for miles. It doesn't need much air. It finds it through cracks, old shafts and holes.
{a:think/suspicious} Officials tried to dig it out. They tried to flood it. They tried to fill the tunnels. Every attempt was too small, too slow, or ran out of money.
{a:shrug/deadpan} Fighting an underground coal fire with a few trucks of dirt is like trying to stop a forest fire by politely asking it to leave.

{a:point_up/neutral} Documented. A fire started in the town's coal mines around nineteen sixty two. Efforts to stop it failed. After a boy nearly died in a sinkhole in nineteen eighty one, the government paid to relocate the town. Its ZIP code was revoked in two thousand two. The fire still burns.
