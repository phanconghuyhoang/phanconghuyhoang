Legend says the Mary Celeste was found with warm tea and half-eaten meals on the table. The men who boarded her in 1872 described a wet, messy ship with no such thing. The creepy details came from Arthur Conan Doyle's 1884 short story "J. Habakuk Jephson's Statement", which renamed her the Marie Celeste.
