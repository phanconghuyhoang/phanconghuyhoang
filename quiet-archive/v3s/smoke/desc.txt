On 2 July 1937 the US Coast Guard cutter Itasca waited at Howland Island, making smoke so Amelia Earhart could spot it. At 7:42 a.m. she radioed: "We must be on you but cannot see you. But gas is running low." The Itasca could hear her, but she could not hear the ship. She and navigator Fred Noonan were never found.
