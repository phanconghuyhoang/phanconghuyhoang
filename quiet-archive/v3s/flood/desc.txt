When the Mary Celeste was brought into Gibraltar in 1872, Attorney General Frederick Solly-Flood suspected the crew had murdered the captain and his family, citing stains on a sword and marks on the bow. Tests found the stains were not blood, and a naval inspection judged the bow marks natural wear. The ten people aboard were never found.
