In 1981, 12-year-old Todd Domboski fell into a sinkhole that opened in his grandmother's backyard in Centralia, Pennsylvania. His cousin pulled him out. The steam rising from the hole contained a lethal level of carbon monoxide from the coal mine fire that had been burning beneath the town since about 1962. The fire is still burning today.
