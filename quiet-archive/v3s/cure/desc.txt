In the summer of 1518 dozens, perhaps hundreds, of people in Strasbourg danced uncontrollably for weeks. Physicians blamed "hot blood" and prescribed more dancing, so the council opened guild halls, built stages in the horse and grain markets and hired musicians. It made things worse, and the council then banned public dancing and most music.
