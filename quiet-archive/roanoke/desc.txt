In 1587 about 115 English men, women and children were left on Roanoke Island. Their governor, John White, sailed to England for supplies and could not return for three years. When he did, on his granddaughter Virginia Dare's third birthday, the settlement was empty, and the only message was one carved word: CROATOAN. Archie opens the file on America's oldest unsolved mystery and separates the record from the legend.

DOCUMENTED vs LEGEND
Documented: about 115 colonists landed on Roanoke in 1587; White left on 27 Aug 1587; the Armada ban and a 1588 attack on his ships delayed him; on 18 Aug 1590 he found the houses dismantled, chests dug up, "CRO" on a tree and "CROATOAN" on a post, and no agreed distress cross; a storm and a lost anchor stopped him searching Croatoan; finds on Hatteras Island and at "Site X" are debated.
Legend: that the colonists left no clue; the Dare Stones (shown to be fakes in 1941); anything supernatural.
