{a:point_up/smug}{t:HOW TO CHECK A GHOST SHIP} So here's a little toolkit, so you can check the next ghost ship story yourself.
{a:talk/neutral} Step one. Does the ship have a registry entry, a builder, or an owner? Real ships leave paperwork from the day they're built.
{a:talk/neutral} Step two. Is there a first source, written at the time, by someone who was there? Or does every version say, according to reports?
{a:talk/neutral} Step three. Do the details stay the same between versions? Real events get blurrier over time. Legends get more specific.
{a:lean_in/suspicious} Step four. Does the ending destroy all the evidence? Sinking, burning, vanishing. If the story conveniently can't be checked, that's not proof it's false. But it is a reason to be careful.
{a:shrug/deadpan} Run the Ourang Medan through that list, and it fails almost every step. Which, honestly, is impressive. It takes real effort to be that mysterious.
