DOCUMENTED vs LEGEND
Documented: versions of the story appeared in newspapers in 1940 (including Il Piccolo in Trieste and British papers credited to the Associated Press) and again in 1948 (De Locomotief in the Dutch East Indies, an American paper), and in the US Coast Guard's Proceedings of the Merchant Marine Council in 1952; the details, dates and location change between versions.
Not found: any registry entry, builder or owner for a ship named Ourang Medan, or any record of the rescue in the Silver Star's logs.
Legend: the frozen faces, the dog, the smuggled cargo, the last words "I die" and the explosion that destroyed the evidence. Proposed explanations (smuggled chemicals, nerve agent, carbon monoxide) remain speculation.
