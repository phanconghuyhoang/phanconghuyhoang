On 5 December 1945 five US Navy TBM Avenger bombers, Flight 19, left Fort Lauderdale on a training flight and never came back. Fourteen men were lost, and a PBM Mariner sent to search for them vanished with 13 more. Archie opens the Navy's own records to separate what happened from the Bermuda Triangle legend it created.

DOCUMENTED vs LEGEND
Documented: 5 Avengers and 14 airmen lost on "Navigation problem No. 1"; Lt. Charles Taylor reported compass failure and believed he was over the Florida Keys while over the Bahamas; the flight ran low on fuel at night over rough seas; PBM-5 BuNo 59225 (13 men) was lost after a 19:30 radio call and the SS Gaines Mills reported a fire on the sea; the Navy's finding was later changed to "causes or reasons unknown".
Legend: strange glowing water, mysterious final messages, alien abduction and a triangle that "eats" aircraft. The term "Bermuda Triangle" was coined by Vincent Gaddis in 1964.
