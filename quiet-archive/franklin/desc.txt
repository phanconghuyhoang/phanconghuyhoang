DOCUMENTED vs LEGEND
Documented: 129 men; ships beset off King William Island on 12 Sept 1846; Franklin died 11 June 1847; ships deserted 22 April 1848 with 105 survivors under Crozier heading for Back's Fish River; no survivors; Inuit accounts (incl. of cannibalism) confirmed by forensic study; Erebus found 2014, Terror 2016; James Fitzjames identified by DNA in 2024.
Legend: a cursed expedition, ghost ships, and the Victorian claim that Inuit witnesses could not be believed.
