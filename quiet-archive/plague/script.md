## s13 | plague/bg/board.png | story
{cam:0.5,0.45,1.0,1.04,0,0}{t:THEORY 1: POISON} So what really happened? Modern theories. Theory one. Ergot poisoning.
{a:talk/neutral} Ergot is a fungus that grows on damp rye. It contains chemicals related to LSD, and it can cause hallucinations and convulsions.
{a:shrug/deadpan} Sounds perfect. One problem. Ergot also cuts off blood flow to your arms and legs. People poisoned with it could barely walk, never mind dance for days.
{a:point_up/neutral}{t:THEORY 2: MASS PANIC} Theory two. The one most historians favour today, argued by historian John Waller.
{a:talk/neutral} Mass psychogenic illness. Extreme stress, hunger and fear, plus a shared belief in a dancing curse, pushed people into a kind of trance. And trances spread.
{a:point_l/suspicious} It sounds strange, but it has happened in modern times too. In nineteen sixty two, a laughing epidemic in Tanganyika, now Tanzania, spread through schools and villages for months.
