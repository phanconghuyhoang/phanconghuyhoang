DOCUMENTED vs LEGEND
Documented: found 4 Dec 1872 by the Dei Gratia; about 3.5 ft of water in the hold; lifeboat, navigation instruments and most papers missing; six months of provisions and personal belongings left; last log entry 25 Nov 1872 off Santa Maria, Azores; 9 of 1,701 barrels of alcohol empty at Genoa; the Gibraltar inquiry found no evidence of violence; the ship was deliberately wrecked off Haiti in 1885.
Legend: warm tea or half-eaten meals, a ship's cat, the name "Marie Celeste" and the mutiny from Arthur Conan Doyle's 1884 story "J. Habakuk Jephson's Statement".
