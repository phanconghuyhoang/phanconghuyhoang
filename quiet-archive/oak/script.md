## s05 | room | host
{a:point_up/suspicious}{t:WAIT A MINUTE} But here's the first problem. That story wasn't written down in seventeen ninety five.
{a:talk/neutral} The earliest published accounts of the money pit appeared in the eighteen fifties, more than sixty years later. By then, the story had been told and retold around a lot of kitchen tables.
{a:hands_hips/neutral} So some of the most famous details, the oak tree, the pulley, the perfect platforms, may be real, may be exaggerated, or may be inventions. We simply don't have the original records.
