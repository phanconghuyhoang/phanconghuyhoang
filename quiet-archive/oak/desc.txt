DOCUMENTED vs LEGEND
Documented: digging since at least the early 1800s; flooded shafts; failed companies; FDR's 1909 investment; six deaths, four of them in 1965; a few old finds such as coconut fibre and coins.
Legend: perfect log platforms every ten feet, the decoded "two million pounds" stone (now lost), booby-trapped flood tunnels, and every treasure theory from Captain Kidd to Marie Antoinette.
